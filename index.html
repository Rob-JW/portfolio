About

British Army Warrant Officer Class 1 (ASM, REME), 24 years in defence engineering. Cyber security sits outside my core Knowledge, Skills and Experience (KSE) — I’m approaching it with the principles that carry through command: Focused Learning, Discipline, and Consistency.
